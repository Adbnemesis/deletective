# Deletective search strategy and keyword evidence

Research date: 28 October 2026. Goal: help people find a genuinely playable fictional-message comedy game, then reach a first round quickly. This is a qualitative prelaunch strategy, not a claim of traffic, rankings, search volume or keyword difficulty.

## Evidence and measurement boundaries

The available web search was used for discovery and intent inspection. Queries covered online party games, online writing games with friends, two-player browser games without download, long-distance couples games, group-chat/texting games and deleted-message games, plus competitor-specific searches. Returned pages are examples observed in this session, **not an independently verified Google top-ten export or stable rank order**. Location, personalization, engine and time can change results.

No Search Console/Bing property is connected; no keyword-planner account or paid Ahrefs/Semrush/DataForSEO dataset was available. Consequently monthly global volume, country volumes, numeric keyword difficulty, competitor DA/backlink counts, current rankings and trend direction are **Unknown for every candidate below**. No metric is inferred from snippet count, wording or popularity of another game. The installed Ian skill provides a future data path, not credentials or an entitlement to paid data.

Observed primary product sources:

- [Gartic Phone](https://garticphone.com/), [skribbl.io](https://skribbl.io/), [Make It Meme](https://makeitmeme.com/) and [Punchline](https://punchline.party/) explain their game and expose play entry points. They show that playable tools, understandable rules and easy invitations are the relevant experience for category searches.
- [Curio's two-player guide](https://curioroom.net/blog/two-player-browser-games-no-download) discusses separate-device links and phone/laptop compatibility. [ToucanPlay](https://toucanplay.games/) and [2play.lol](https://2play.lol/no-download-games) show broad two-player game portfolios. A single text-comedy game has narrower scope.
- [Here We Ask](https://hereweask.com/) offers conversation cards and daily content; [Join Party Games](https://www.joinpartygames.com/) targets friends/couples and separates actual live features from its roadmap. Deletective should clearly distinguish fictional jokes from relationship questions.
- Exact concept searches returned noisy material about deleted-chat recovery and unrelated software. [A 2011 public thread](https://groups.google.com/g/the-stream-of-consciousness/c/1H7Nhlr4Afs) uses the phrase “Guess The Deleted Message”; it proves prior wording, not a measured search market. Do not assume strong demand for the phrase.

## Positioning and initial priority

“Read the reactions. Invent the missing message. Reveal together.” is the core product explanation. Search copy can add “free online party game”, “for two players” or “for couples” where the relevant mode supplies that promise. It must not imply authentic private-message recovery, AI judging, a public chat service, matchmaking or a relationship assessment.

Priority means **qualitative product fit**, not estimated rankability. High priority: explicit two-person no-download play, fictional chat comedy and short group writing games. Medium: broad social/party intent and date-night use cases with honest context. Low: broad “free multiplayer” and “2 player games” head terms that may primarily want arcade/board games or local shared-keyboard play.

## Public page architecture

These are implementation requirements; the final URL list and actual checks are in `seo-audit.md`.

| Route | Main audience/action | Content that makes this page useful |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `/` | Understand and start | Animated/interactive fictional chat, one-sentence loop, friend/Duo/Daily entry, accurate free/no-account statement |
| `/play` | Create/join | Name/avatar, mode, room code or invite link; direct entry with errors and accessible controls |
| `/2-player` | Exactly two people | Secret simultaneous answers, no biased two-player voting, shared score, invitation steps, meaningful example and launch |
| `/couples` | Pair/date night | Fictional tailored content, optional packs, no personal disclosures, clear separate-device instructions and launch |
| `/party` | 3–10 people | Classic votes, no self-vote, ties, Chain explanation, host settings, invitation/privacy guidance |
| `/daily` | Try solo | UTC-dated prompt with local completion/streak, no login; curated example only after answer |
| `/how-to-play` | Learn with minimal friction | Worked round, timing, counts, scoring and differences between modes; links directly to play |
| `/privacy`, `/terms` | Understand handling/rules | Actual retention/local storage, no automatic publication; provisional legal text requires owner approval |
| `/room` and room-code paths | Existing guests only | `noindex` in HTML/HTTP; no credential or user answer in sitemap/schema/social metadata |

Home targets the broad product definition; Duo and Couples own their use cases. The play form is task-focused rather than a duplicate long homepage. Extra guides should exist only if they resolve a distinct question people actually have. Private answers and generated cards never become indexable content libraries.

## Technical implementation requirements

- Prebuild meaningful public HTML. Public rules, examples, titles and descriptions must survive JavaScript being disabled. Load the interactive game only where needed.
- Use one validated `PUBLIC_SITE_URL` for canonical, OG URLs and sitemap. Development should use a local origin with noindex controls; no unowned candidate domain is a canonical target.
- Unique page titles/descriptions, one clear primary heading, semantic sections and descriptive internal links. Length checks are lint hints, not Google pass/fail rules.
- Accurate `WebApplication`/`VideoGame`/`Game` semantics as applicable, with actual free pricing and browser support. No fabricated ratings, awards, downloads or review counts. Validate JSON syntax and matching visible facts; no rich-result promise.
- Return an actual 404 for unknown pages. Exclude API, room, private state and share results from sitemap. `robots.txt` should reference only a real configured sitemap. `noindex` is not authentication.
- Keep the social preview original, lightweight and readable; canonical images use actual configured origin. Share exports do not include room tokens or invite codes.
- Prioritize keyboard/touch controls, readable text and visible errors. Measure render/load behavior; reserve space for graphics to reduce layout shift. Distinguish lab measurements from production Core Web Vitals.
- Follow [Google's official AI-feature guidance](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/appearance/ai-features): regular SEO fundamentals, textual facts and matching structured data; no special AI file requirement or guaranteed indexing. Crawler search access and model-training policy are separate decisions.

## Growth and measurement after authorization

Start with useful share cards and rematches, then invite consenting playtest groups. Do not spam communities or auto-message anyone. A single room is not a new SEO page. No public comparison article is warranted until it has tested facts and substantial unique value.

Before launch, the owner must connect/verify Google Search Console and Bing Webmaster Tools, choose a domain and approve legal notices. Sitemap submission and indexing are not claimed complete. Cloudflare Web Analytics can be added with the owner's actual site token; no token or account is currently assumed. Do not place room URLs, codes, names, guest tokens or submissions into analytics. Use coarse page/mode/event names with minimized metadata.

Useful funnel: public visit → mode entry → room created → invited friend joins → game starts → round completes → session completes → rematch/share. Report rates with denominators and time windows; no synthetic popularity counters. Compare Duo/Couples/Party only with adequate sample size. Review organic clicks/impressions, query intent, indexed canonical count and errors by device/country once real data exists.

Next research step: obtain owner-provided read-only keyword exports or approved tool access for the top 10–20 product-fit phrases. Collect global and India/US/UK volumes, actual localized SERPs and trends with dates; do not assume those markets behave the same. Use the numbers to prioritize useful pages, not to justify hundreds of variants. Revisit at 28 and 90 days after sufficient traffic, with no guaranteed ranking or revenue target.
